# Tree spec: Trigonometry

Data: `web/src/data-trigonometry.js` (29 nodes, 8 eras; ids `trig-*`, all `planned` until written). Pages `web/content/trigonometry/<id>.js`, checks `checks/trigonometry/<id>.py`, one lab file per batch `web/labs/trig-b<n>.js`. Writers read only `web/WRITER-PACK-MATH.md` + their batch below.
Standard: OpenStax *Algebra and Trigonometry 2e* ch. 7–10 (7 The Unit Circle, 8 Periodic Functions, 9 Trigonometric Identities and Equations, 10 Further Applications) with *Precalculus 2e* §6.x/7.6 for sinusoidal modelling and harmonic motion; terminology as in a college Trigonometry course (Lial *Trigonometry*). `grade: "Grade 11–12 · college Trigonometry"`. `voice: "plain"`. Angles in degrees **and** radians throughout; radian answers exact (π/6, 5π/4) unless a decimal is asked for.
Excluded (other fields): parametric equations and their graphs, conic sections in polar form, matrices (Precalculus); calculus of trig functions (Calculus I).
Already taught (link, don't re-teach): **g-trig-ratios** (sin, cos, tan of an acute angle, SOH-CAH-TOA, solving right triangles, elevation/depression, sin⁻¹/cos⁻¹/tan⁻¹ of acute angles, sin²θ + cos²θ = 1 for acute θ, sin θ = cos(90° − θ)); g-special-right (30-60-90, 45-45-90); g-circle-measure (circumference, arc length and sector area in degrees); g-circle-equations; a2-transformations, a2-inverses, a2-complex-ops, a2-rational-func, a2-exp-func.

**trig-six-ratios** The Six Trigonometric Ratios & Special Angles — pre g-trig-ratios, g-special-right — unlocks trig-any-angle. csc, sec, cot as reciprocals; all six from a right triangle; exact values at 30°, 45°, 60° from the special triangles; cofunction identities for all six pairs; finding all six from one ratio (sketch a triangle); two-triangle problems (height from two angles of elevation). Lab **F**: right triangle with draggable vertex; *Ratios* (six ratios live, reciprocal pairs linked); *Special* (snap to 30/45/60, exact values build from the 1:√3:2 and 1:1:√2 sides, guarded table fill); *Cofunction* (swap viewpoint to the other acute angle). Keys as field-wide; reciprocal ratios dashed in the same colour.
**trig-radians** Radian Measure, Arc Length & Sector Area — pre trig-angles, g-circle-measure — unlocks trig-angular-speed, trig-unit-circle. A radian as the angle whose arc equals the radius; π rad = 180°; converting both ways; exact radian multiples; s = rθ and A = ½r²θ (θ in radians); why the formulas need radians. Lab **D**: circle of adjustable radius; *Wrap* (lay radius-length arcs around the circle, 2π ≈ 6.28 fit); *Convert* (degree ↔ radian stepper, guarded); *Arc & sector* (drag θ and r, s and A live). Keys: θ c1, arc s c3, radius c4, sector area c5.
### Era 2 · The Circular Functions
**trig-angular-speed** Linear & Angular Speed — pre trig-radians — unlocks trig-modeling. ω = θ/t, v = s/t = rω, rpm → rad/s, gears/belts/wheels (same v at the contact, different ω), Earth's rotation, bicycle problems. Lab **E**: two linked wheels (belt or gear) with radii sliders; *Spin* (ω and v live; dots on each rim race); *Convert* (rpm → rad/s → v, stepper, guarded); *Bike* (pedal gear, wheel gear, wheel radius → speed). Keys: ω c1, linear speed v c5, radius c4, rim points c2/c3.
**trig-unit-circle** The Unit Circle — pre trig-radians, g-trig-ratios, g-special-right, g-circle-equations — unlocks trig-any-angle, trig-sin-cos-graphs. Sine and cosine as the coordinates (cos t, sin t) of the terminal point for real t; domain and range; the special points at multiples of π/6 and π/4 by symmetry; tan t = y/x; sin²t + cos²t = 1 from x² + y² = 1; values at quadrantal angles. Lab **D · Unit circle**: drag the point; *Explore* (projections, exact values when snapped); *Build* (fill first-quadrant values, reflect to other quadrants, guarded); *Quiz* (point → angle and angle → point, exact). Keys field-wide.
**trig-any-angle** Trigonometric Functions of Any Angle — pre trig-unit-circle, trig-six-ratios — unlocks trig-fundamental-ids, trig-law-sines, trig-vectors, trig-polar-coords. The six functions from a point (x, y) on the terminal side, r = √(x² + y²); signs by quadrant (ASTC), reference angles in degrees and radians, evaluating exactly with reference angle + sign, undefined values, finding all six from one value and a quadrant. Lab **D**: drag (x, y) anywhere; *Point* (r, six values, sign chart by quadrant); *Reference* (stepper: quadrant → reference angle → value → sign, guarded); *Given one* (sin θ = −3/5, θ in QIII → all six). Keys field-wide; reference angle c1 dashed.
**trig-fundamental-ids** Fundamental Identities — pre trig-any-angle — unlocks trig-other-graphs, trig-verify-ids, trig-equations. Reciprocal, quotient and Pythagorean identities (all three forms), even/odd identities, periodicity (2π, π for tan/cot), cofunction in radians; using them to find values and simplify expressions to sines and cosines. Lab **C**: *Pythagorean* (unit circle triangle → divide by cos², sin² to get the other two, animated); *Even/Odd* (θ and −θ mirrored); *Simplify* (stepper rewrites an expression, guarded result). Keys field-wide.
### Era 3 · Graphs & Inverses
**trig-sin-cos-graphs** Graphs of Sine & Cosine — pre trig-unit-circle — unlocks trig-sinusoids, trig-other-graphs. Lab **D**: unit circle on the left, graph on the right, dragging t traces both (y-projection → sin graph; x-projection rotated → cos graph); *Key points* (plot the 5 points, guarded); *Compare* (sin vs cos shift). Keys: t c1, cos c2, sin c3.
**trig-sinusoids** Amplitude, Period, Phase Shift & Midline — pre trig-sin-cos-graphs, a2-transformations — unlocks trig-modeling, trig-polar-graphs. y = A sin(B(x − C)) + D and the cosine form: amplitude |A|, period 2π/|B|, phase shift C, midline y = D, range; writing an equation from a graph; key points of one period. Lab **A**: sliders A, B, C, D over the dashed parent; *Explore*; *Match* (target curve, guarded parameters); *From graph* (read max/min/period off a curve, stepper). Keys: A c3, B c2, C c1, D c4, parent c5 dashed.
**trig-other-graphs** Graphs of Tangent, Cotangent, Secant & Cosecant — pre trig-sin-cos-graphs, trig-fundamental-ids, a2-rational-func — unlocks trig-inverse. tan x = sin x / cos x: asymptotes where cos x = 0, period π; cot; sec and csc as reciprocals of cos and sin (U-shaped branches touching the sine/cosine curve at its peaks); transformations y = A tan(B(x − C)) + D. Lab **B**: function select; ghost sin/cos dashed with the reciprocal/quotient built pointwise; asymptotes violet; *Transform* (A, B, C sliders, period and asymptote readout, guarded in Match). Keys: sin c3, cos c2, result c5, asymptotes c4.
**trig-modeling** Sinusoidal Models & Harmonic Motion — pre trig-sinusoids, trig-angular-speed, a2-exp-func — unlocks none. Fitting y = A sin(B(t − C)) + D to data (tides, daylight hours, temperature, Ferris wheel height); simple harmonic motion d = a sin ωt or a cos ωt, frequency ω/2π, period; damped motion a·e^(−ct) sin ωt. Lab **E**: *Ferris wheel* (rider height from rotation, graph traced live); *Fit* (scatter of monthly data, drag max/min/period handles, model readout, guarded parameters in the Your-turn data set); *Spring* (mass on spring with damping slider, envelope ±ae^(−ct) dashed). **trig-inverse** Inverse Trigonometric Functions — pre trig-other-graphs, a2-inverses — unlocks trig-equations, trig-vectors, trig-polar-coords. Restricting domains to make sin, cos, tan one-to-one; sin⁻¹ (arcsin) on [−1, 1] → [−π/2, π/2], cos⁻¹ → [0, π], tan⁻¹ → (−π/2, π/2) (arcsec/arccsc/arccot defined, briefly); graphs as reflections in y = x; exact values; compositions sin(sin⁻¹ x), sin⁻¹(sin x) (when they don't cancel), and cos(tan⁻¹ x) via a triangle. Lab **D**: *Restrict* (drag the restriction window on sin x; the horizontal line test passes; the mirrored inverse grows); *Evaluate* (unit circle: the answer must lie in the principal range, a wrong-quadrant answer shown and rejected, guarded); *Compose* (triangle method stepper). Keys: f c3 (sin) / c2 (cos) / c5 (tan), inverse c1, y = x c4.
### Era 4 · Identities
**trig-verify-ids** Verifying Trigonometric Identities — pre trig-fundamental-ids, a1-rational-add — unlocks trig-sum-difference. Identity vs conditional equation; strategies: work one side, rewrite in sines and cosines, combine fractions, factor, multiply by a conjugate, use Pythagorean forms; why you may not "cross over"; a graph test shows equality but does not prove it. Lab **C**: *Verify* (pick an identity; stepper transforms the left side, each step names its rule, guarded final form); *Graph test* (both sides plotted; a non-identity separates); *Your turn* (choose the next rule from 3 options, MathRules checks). Keys: left side c1, right side c2, rule used c4, done c5.
**trig-sum-difference** Sum & Difference Formulas — pre trig-verify-ids — unlocks trig-double-half, trig-sum-product, trig-complex-polar. cos(α ± β), sin(α ± β), tan(α ± β); proof of cos(α − β) by the distance formula on the unit circle; exact values like cos 15°, sin 105°, tan(7π/12); cofunction identities as consequences; evaluating with given values in quadrants; verifying identities with them. Lab **D · Unit circle**: two angles α, β; *Proof* (two chords of equal length; stepper through the distance-formula proof); *Exact* (split 75° = 45° + 30°, guarded); *Given values* (sin α = 3/5 in QII etc.). Keys: α c1, β c2, α ± β c5, chords c4.
**trig-double-half** Double-Angle, Half-Angle & Power-Reducing Formulas — pre trig-sum-difference — unlocks trig-equations-multi. sin 2θ, the three forms of cos 2θ, tan 2θ; power-reducing formulas; half-angle formulas with the sign chosen by the quadrant of θ/2; exact values (sin 22.5°); projectile range R = v²sin 2θ/g as an application. Lab **C**: *Double* (graph sin 2x vs 2 sin x to kill the classic error; derive from sum formula, stepper); *Half* (pick θ and quadrant, stepper picks the sign of θ/2, guarded); *Range* (launch angle slider, maximum at 45°). Keys: θ c1, 2θ c5, θ/2 c2, sign c3.
**trig-sum-product** Product-to-Sum & Sum-to-Product Formulas — pre trig-sum-difference — unlocks none. Deriving product-to-sum from the sum/difference formulas; sum-to-product; using them to evaluate and simplify; beats: sin a + sin b as a slow envelope times a fast wave (sound, tuning). Lab **D**: *Derive* (add the two sum/difference lines, stepper); *Beats* (two frequency sliders, sum graphed with envelope 2cos((a−b)x/2) dashed, optional audio through the music audio kit if available); *Evaluate* (sin 75° cos 15°, guarded). Keys: first wave c2, second wave c3, sum c5, envelope c4.
### Era 5 · Trigonometric Equations
**trig-equations** Solving Trigonometric Equations — pre trig-inverse, trig-fundamental-ids, a1-quad-factor — unlocks trig-equations-multi. Linear-type (2 sin x − 1 = 0), all solutions in [0, 2π) and the general solution (+ 2πn, + πn for tan), quadratic-type (2cos²x − cos x − 1 = 0, factoring or the quadratic formula), using a Pythagorean identity to get one function, square-both-sides with extraneous checks, calculator answers with the second solution from the reference angle. Lab **C+graph**: *Circle* (horizontal line y = k cuts the unit circle in 0, 1 or 2 points, both angles shown); *Graph* (y = sin x and y = k on [0, 2π), intersections, general solution ladder); *Steps* (problem picker, stepper, guarded solution set). Keys: k c3, solutions c5, reference angle c1, period ladder c4.
**trig-ambiguous** The Ambiguous Case (SSA) — pre trig-law-sines — unlocks none. Given a, b and A: compare a with the height h = b sin A; no triangle, one right triangle, two triangles (B and 180° − B), one triangle (a ≥ b); acute vs obtuse A; solving both triangles. Lab **F**: *Swing* (side a swings as a compass arc from C; it misses, touches, cuts twice or once; readout of the case and h); *Solve* (stepper finds B, tests 180° − B, solves both, guarded); *Classify* drill. Keys: given A c1, side b c2, swinging side a c3, height h c4, solutions c5.
**trig-equations-multi** Equations with Multiple Angles & Identities — pre trig-equations, trig-double-half — unlocks none. sin 2x = k, tan 3x = k (solve for the multiple angle over the stretched interval, then divide), equations needing double-angle or sum identities (sin 2x = cos x, cos 2x = sin x), factoring after an identity, approximate solutions by graph. Lab **C+graph**: *Stretch* (interval [0, 2π) for x becomes [0, 4π) for 2x; solutions counted on both axes); *Identity* (stepper: rewrite → factor → solve each factor, guarded); *Graph* (intersection reader for non-algebraic cases). Keys: multiple angle kx c1, x c2, solutions c5.
### Era 6 · Oblique Triangles
**trig-law-sines** The Law of Sines — pre trig-any-angle, g-triangle-angles — unlocks trig-ambiguous, trig-law-cosines. Oblique triangles; a/sin A = b/sin B = c/sin C proved with an altitude (acute and obtuse cases); solving AAS and ASA; bearings (N 35° E and 3-digit), surveying and navigation problems; when the law of sines is not enough (SAS, SSS). Lab **F**: *Proof* (drop the altitude, h = b sin A = a sin B, stepper); *Solve* (given parts outlined, found parts appear one per step, guarded); *Bearing* (two observers sight a fire, triangle from bearings). Keys: angle A c1, side a c3, side b c2, altitude c4, found parts c5.
**trig-law-cosines** The Law of Cosines — pre trig-law-sines, g-pythagorean — unlocks trig-triangle-area, trig-vector-apps, trig-dot-product. c² = a² + b² − 2ab cos C, proved with coordinates; Pythagorean Theorem as the case C = 90°; solving SAS and SSS; finding the largest angle first (cos⁻¹ gives obtuse angles directly); which law to use for each case. Lab **F**: *Slide C* (angle C slider; the correction −2ab cos C as a signed area/length, zero at 90°); *Solve* (SAS/SSS stepper, guarded); *Which law?* (case → law drill). Keys: C c1, a c2, b c3, c c5, correction term c4.
**trig-triangle-area** Area of a Triangle: SAS & Heron's Formula — pre trig-law-cosines, g-area-polygons — unlocks none. Area = ½ab sin C from ½·base·height; Heron's formula with s = (a + b + c)/2 (from the law of cosines; proof sketch); area for AAS/ASA via the law of sines first; land plots, sails. Lab **F**: *SAS* (angle slider; the height b sin C shown; area max at 90°); *Heron* (three side sliders, triangle-inequality guard, stepper for s and the root); *Plot* (irregular quadrilateral split by a diagonal). Keys: C c1, height c4, area c5, sides c2/c3.
### Era 7 · Vectors
**trig-vectors** Vectors: Magnitude, Direction & Components — pre trig-inverse, trig-any-angle — unlocks trig-vector-apps, trig-dot-product. Directed segments, equal vectors, component form ⟨a, b⟩, magnitude, direction angle (tan⁻¹ with the quadrant fixed), from magnitude and angle to components (‖v‖cos θ, ‖v‖sin θ), i and j, unit vectors, addition (tip-to-tail and parallelogram), scalar multiples, subtraction. **trig-vector-apps** Applications of Vectors — pre trig-vectors, trig-law-cosines — unlocks none. Lab **E**: *Plane & wind* (heading, airspeed, wind sliders; ground vector and course readout, guarded in Your turn); *Equilibrium* (hang a weight from two cables, tensions); *Incline* (weight split into components). **trig-dot-product** The Dot Product & Projections — pre trig-vectors, trig-law-cosines — unlocks none. u · v = u₁v₁ + u₂v₂, properties, u · v = ‖u‖‖v‖cos θ (proof from the law of cosines), angle between vectors, orthogonal vectors, scalar and vector projection, decomposition into parallel and orthogonal parts, work W = F · d. ### Era 8 · Polar Coordinates & Complex Numbers
**trig-polar-coords** Polar Coordinates — pre trig-inverse, trig-any-angle — unlocks trig-polar-graphs, trig-complex-polar. (r, θ), negative r, many names for one point, conversion both ways (x = r cos θ, y = r sin θ; r² = x² + y², tan θ = y/x with the quadrant fixed), converting equations (r = 4 sin θ ↔ x² + (y − 2)² = 4, x = 3 ↔ r = 3 sec θ). Lab **D**: polar grid; *Plot* (drag (r, θ) incl. negative r, all names listed); *Convert* (stepper both directions, guarded); *Equations* (rectangular and polar forms of a circle/line overlaid). **trig-complex-polar** Complex Numbers in Polar Form — pre trig-polar-coords, trig-sum-difference, a2-complex-ops — unlocks trig-de-moivre. Modulus and argument, z = r(cos θ + i sin θ) = r cis θ, converting both ways, product rule (multiply moduli, add arguments; proof by the sum formulas), quotient rule. Lab **D**: complex plane (`k.cplane`); *Polar form* (drag z: r and θ, exact when snapped); *Multiply* (z·w as rotate-and-scale, angles add visibly); *Divide* (guarded). Keys: z c1, w c2, result c5, modulus c4, argument c3.
**trig-de-moivre** De Moivre's Theorem & Complex Roots — pre trig-complex-polar — unlocks none. Lab **D**: *Powers* (repeated multiplication spiral, slider n); *Roots* (n and c sliders; regular n-gon of roots, each root's argument (θ + 2πk)/n, guarded in Your turn); *Unity* (roots of unity, their sum is 0). **trig-polar-graphs** Graphs of Polar Equations — pre trig-polar-coords, trig-sinusoids — unlocks none. Plotting by tables, symmetry tests (polar axis, θ = π/2, pole), circles r = a cos θ / a sin θ, cardioids and limaçons r = a ± b cos θ (inner loop, dimple, convex), rose curves r = a cos nθ (n or 2n petals), lemniscates, spirals; max |r| and zeros. Keys: θ c1, r c4, curve c5, negative-r part c3.
